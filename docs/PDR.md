# Product Definition Report (PDR)

Smart Restaurant Operations & POS Platform

## Version
1.0

## Prepared By
Prince Movaliya

## Purpose
This document defines the product vision, objectives, scope, business value, target users, and strategic direction of the Smart Restaurant Operations & POS Platform.

## Product Vision
To create a scalable restaurant operations platform that unifies billing, kitchen coordination, ingredient inventory, procurement, reporting, and predictive analytics in one product.

## Product Background
The starting concept includes a backend admin panel, a POS terminal, and a Kitchen Display System. The admin configures products, employees, tables, payment methods, and promotions. The cashier operates the POS, and the kitchen processes live orders from a dedicated display. The product is expanded here to include stock intelligence, vendor management, reorder planning, and AI-enhanced decision support so it appears as a full final-year product, not just a simple POS.

## Problem Statement
Traditional cafe POS systems usually solve only billing and order handling. Restaurants still face issues such as:
- No visibility into raw-material usage
- Sudden stock shortages
- Manual purchase tracking
- Poor coordination between cash counter and kitchen
- Limited reporting for operational decisions
- No prediction for future demand

## Product Goal
To build a generalized restaurant operations system that is suitable for:
- Academic final-year demonstration
- Real-world restaurant workflow management
- Future commercialization as a sellable software product

- A complete order-to-kitchen-to-payment flow works successfully
- Ingredient stock updates automatically
- Low-stock alerting works correctly
- Purchase workflow is functional

- AI recommendations are generated with meaningful logic
- The project is perceived as advanced and differentiated

## Risks
- Large scope for final-year timeline
- AI results depend on available historical data
- Real-time system behavior requires careful testing
- Inventory logic can become complex if not designed early

## Risk Mitigation
- Build in phases
- Keep MVP stable first
- Use rule-based fallback for AI modules
- Use modular architecture
- Keep documentation strong from day one
